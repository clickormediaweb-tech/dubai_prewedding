FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

General:

I specialize in capturing pre-wedding photos in Dubai’s most iconic locations, from the majestic desert dunes and stunning beaches to the luxurious cityscape and historical souks. However, I’m open to exploring new locations that resonate with your love story.

My packages vary depending on the duration of the shoot, number of locations, and edited photos delivered. Please Schedule a call for details, or contact me for a custom quote.

I blend documentary and creative styles, capturing genuine emotions and candid moments alongside artistic compositions that showcase the beauty of Dubai and your love story.

Booking & Process:

The easiest way is to contact me through the website's contact form or send me an email. We can discuss your vision, answer any questions, and check my availability. Once confirmed, I'll send you a contract and booking details.

Before the shoot, we'll have a consultation to discuss your ideas, preferred locations, outfits, and any special requests. On the shoot day, I'll guide you through everything, capturing natural moments and creating beautiful photos that reflect your love story.

Additional Services:

While I don’t directly rent these vehicles myself, I’ve built strong relationships with reputable local companies specializing in yacht and sports car rentals. I’d be happy to connect you with them based on your needs and budget to ensure a seamless experience for your pre-wedding shoot.

I can connect you with some of my trusted makeup artist contacts who understand the specific needs of photography.

But I recommend you to do your own makeup and hair for the pre-wedding shoot. This can be a great way to personalize your look and ensure you feel comfortable. Here are some benefits of doing your own makeup and hair:

  • Save Money: This is a significant cost-saving option, especially for couples on a tight budget.
  • Personalized Look: You have complete control over your look and can create something that perfectly complements your style and the shoot location.
  • Confidence Booster: Doing your own makeup and hair can be empowering, knowing you achieved a flawless look yourself.

Some of my packages may include the option to choose from a selection of beautiful long dresses.

I can offer guidance and inspiration based on your desired style and the shoot location.

Deliverables:

You can expect to receive your edited photos & videos within 1-2 weeks after the shoot. I'll deliver them through a secure online gallery for easy downloading and sharing.
